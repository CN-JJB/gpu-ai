## Rubric

Required dimensions are rated:
- NEEDS-EVIDENCE;
- INDEPENDENT;
- TRANSFER.

No weighted score may average away:
- a fatal gate;
- missing material evidence;
- a guessed UNKNOWN;
- an inconsistent final decision.
